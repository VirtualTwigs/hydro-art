# Southeast US Water Data Portals

Research date: 2026-09-15

Water rights, withdrawal permits, and water facility data portals for 12 southeastern US states plus DC. Covers water withdrawal/allocation permits, drinking water programs, wastewater/NPDES permits, groundwater well registries, bulk download availability, and commercial use rights.

---

## Summary of Water Rights Doctrines

All states in this list follow **riparian rights** (reasonable use of water by landowners adjacent to a waterbody), not the prior-appropriation doctrine used in the western US. Most have layered a **registration or permit system** for large withdrawals on top of the common-law riparian base. DC has no independent water rights doctrine -- EPA administers NPDES directly and DC Water/DOEE handle local permitting.
