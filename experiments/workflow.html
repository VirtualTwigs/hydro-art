Buyer proposal becomes an internal request

Beginning state

A customer has a place or question, not a technical project.

They can be thoughtful and discerning while still being unfamiliar with GIS, print specifications, or online ordering. The interface supplies confidence, not a tutorial.

End state

A customer owns an explainable artifact; operations owns a recoverable record.

The final delivery is attributable, reproducible, and connected to the approved brief, proof, and manifest—without exposing operational detail to the buyer.

Internal cataloging / lineage

Keep requested images, produced art, and evidence in their proper places.

Public examples are curated. Customer material is private. Every production derivative retains a traceable parent, brief, recipe, and delivery record.
