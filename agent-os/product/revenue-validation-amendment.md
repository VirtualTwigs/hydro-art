# Revenue Validation Roadmap Amendment

_Decision note · 2026-08-30 · informed by the initial market review_

## Recommendation

Add a gated **Revenue Validation** track before treating the planned watershed
report as a commercial product. Do not remove or rewrite the existing technical
epochs: the core art pipeline is already sufficiently capable to test demand.
The change is a sequencing correction—prove a buyer will pay before expanding
the product surface.

## Proposed insertion: Revenue Validation (4–6 weeks)

Place this short epoch immediately before the next new feature epoch, or run it
in parallel only as a time-boxed commercial experiment. Its outcome is first
revenue, not a self-serve storefront.

1. **Sell a narrow service, not a platform.** Publish an Etsy made-to-order
   listing for a personalized county watershed print in the currently supported
   geography (OR, WA, CA, ID). Deliver a print-ready PDF/PNG in 24–48 hours;
   keep editable SVG and commercial licensing as paid add-ons.
2. **Create a repeatable fulfillment pack.** Add a customer intake form,
   two approved art directions, title/subtitle rules, export checklist, source
   credit, and a proof/approval template. This is an operating recipe, not a
   new pipeline.
3. **Instrument the test.** Track listing views, favorites, inquiries, paid
   orders, fulfillment time, refund rate, requested locations/styles, and net
   revenue after marketplace fees.
4. **Set the gate.** Continue toward catalog/POD and self-serve only after at
   least 8 paid orders or $500 gross revenue within 60 days, with median
   fulfillment under 45 minutes. Otherwise interview 10 non-buyers, revise the
   visual/offer, and run one further test; do not build subscriptions.

## Alignment changes to the current roadmap

- **Keep Epoch 10.** Determinism verification becomes useful commercial
  fulfillment infrastructure, but it is not a prerequisite for the first
  manually reviewed digital order.
- **Keep Epoch 12 as research/product discovery, not near-term revenue.** Its
  model-vs-gauge work is essential for credibility, but it should have a
  commercialization gate after validation results and buyer interviews with
  watershed organizations or consultancies.
- **Add a rights gate to #45–#55.** The PRISM terms state that sale or other
  commercial use requires arrangement with the PRISM Climate Group. Do not sell
  PRISM-derived animations or reports until written permission/licensing is
  documented, or the data dependency is replaced. Record USGS/NHD/WBD source
  versions and attribution separately for every sold art asset.
- **Defer Utah and platform expansion (#47, subscriptions, broad POD catalog)**
  until the offer has passed the revenue gate. Existing four-state scope is
  enough to validate the model.

## Evidence to retain

Save a lightweight order ledger and a monthly decision note in this directory.
The next roadmap decision should use observed conversion and fulfillment data,
not marketplace impressions alone. The market-review source links and pricing
benchmarks belong in `market-analysis/hydro-art-samples.html`'s companion
research, not in product code.
