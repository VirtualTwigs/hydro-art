Data as composition

02 / Systems thinking

Case-study lens

Why the museum print series matters.

It demonstrates a complete design-to-production loop: authentic spatial source, a deliberate visual system, pixel-aware rendering, high-resolution proof, and print-ready vector PDF.

Fine-line control

Target stroke weight is derived from the SVG viewBox and final pixel width, preventing meaningful tributaries from disappearing in a state-scale raster.

Data-led hierarchy

Flow width uses discharge-aware scaling; elevation, watershed membership, and seasonal flow become legible visual variables.

Production resilience

Large SVGs split into watershed layers before rasterization, avoiding renderer node limits and alpha-compositing a complete final image.

Audience-ready evidence

Every featured artifact opens at source quality—native PDF for print, rendered HTML for reports, animated media when present.
