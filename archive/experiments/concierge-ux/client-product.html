What you receive

One museum-grade print.

Archival pigment (giclée) print on 100% cotton rag, acid-free ~310 gsm matte paper.
Pigment inks rated for 100+ years of lightfast color.
Hand-inspected, shipped flat or rolled in a rigid protective tube.
Printed source credit and a provenance manifest tying the print to its data and recipe.

How it stays honest

Expectation, then proof.

Your place is rendered from public-domain USGS hydrography using the direction you choose. You review a private, watermarked proof and can request one adjustment before anything is printed—nothing goes to production without your approval.
